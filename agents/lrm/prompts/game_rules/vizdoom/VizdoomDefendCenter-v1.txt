You stand in the centre of a circular room and cannot move, only turn. Monsters (Demon, MarineChainsawVzd) spawn along the wall and walk toward you; when they reach you they hurt you.

- TURN_LEFT / TURN_RIGHT rotate you in place.
- ATTACK fires your pistol straight ahead. Each shot costs one bullet; you cannot reload.
- Killing a monster scores +1. A new monster spawns to replace it.
- The episode ends when your HEALTH reaches 0 (dying costs one point), or when time runs out.

Things in view are given by their direction from straight ahead (your crosshair) and their distance.
