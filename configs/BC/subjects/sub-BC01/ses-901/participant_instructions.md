# Practice session script (ses-901)

What to say to the participant before each task of `BC_sub-BC01_ses-901_behav.sh`, based on the tasks' READMEs. Buttons are the toolboxes' own mapping (`controls/*-GMPD3-ABXY-nav.json`): **Y = 1, A = 2, X = 3, B = 4**.

**Before you start.** "You'll do five short tasks with the controller, each a couple of minutes long. Between tasks we'll stop, and you can ask me anything. For every task, keep your thumbs ready on the four buttons: **Y, A, X and B**."

## 1. Card sorting with shapes (WCST)

"At the top of the screen there are **four cards**. From left to right, they go with **Y, A, X and B**. These four cards never change.

At the bottom, a **new card** appears on each trial. Your job is to match it with one of the four cards at the top, by pressing that card's button.

There is a **rule** for matching, but I won't tell you what it is. After each answer you'll see a **green check** if you were right, or a **red cross** if you were wrong. Use that to work out the rule.

Be careful: **the rule changes from time to time, without warning.** When you start getting crosses again, the rule has probably changed, so look for the new one.

You have **5 seconds** for each card. At the end you'll see your score and your average time."

*Experimenter note:* don't name the possible rules (colour, shape, number); working them out is the task.

## 2. Card sorting with words (VCST)

"This one works exactly like the last one, but with **words** instead of shapes.

The four words at the top go with **Y, A, X and B**, from left to right. Match each new word at the bottom with one of them. There's a hidden rule, the check or the cross tells you if you're right, and **the rule changes without warning**. Again, 5 seconds per word."

*Experimenter note:* the top words are DIP, POTE, BISON and SOPHIA. The rules are first letter, word length, or category (food, nonword, animal, name). Don't tell the participant.

## 3. Grid puzzles (ARC)

"Each puzzle has three steps.

First, you'll see **two examples**. In each one, a grid of coloured squares on the left is changed into a new grid on the right, always by the **same hidden rule**. Try to figure out the rule. Each example stays up for up to 5 seconds. If you've understood it, you can press **B** to move on.

Then a **test grid** appears, with a proposed answer and a question mark. Decide if the answer follows the rule:

- press **Y** if it's **correct**;
- press **A** if it's **wrong**.

Take at least 2 seconds to look, because presses in the first 2 seconds don't count. You have up to 10 seconds. Then you'll see a check or a cross."

## 4. Spatial working memory (MTB)

"Before each part, a short instruction appears for 5 seconds. Here you'll see a **grid of boxes**. Boxes light up in **four steps**: remember all the boxes that lit up. Sometimes it's one box per step, sometimes two.

Then **two grids** appear side by side, and only one of them shows the boxes that lit up:

- press **A** if it's the **left** grid;
- press **X** if it's the **right** grid.

You have 2 seconds to choose, then a check or a cross. When a cross appears in the middle of the screen, just look at it and keep your head still."

## 5. Mental rotation (MTB)

"You'll see **two 3D shapes** side by side. One is rotated. Decide if they are the **same** object turned around, or **mirror images** of each other:

- press **A** if they're the **same**;
- press **X** if they're **mirror images**.

Answer as soon as you know. You have about 5 seconds per pair, then a check or a cross. When a cross appears in the middle of the screen, just look at it and stay still."

**At the end.** "That's everything. In the scanner, the tasks will be the same but longer, about 7 to 10 minutes each, mixed in with the games. Do you have any questions?"
